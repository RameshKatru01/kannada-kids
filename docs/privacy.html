Privacy policy

Fun Learn – ಕನ್ನಡ · Effective 8 November 2026

In short: a parent creates the account. We keep only what we need to sign you in and to know whether your family has a Premium plan. Your child's progress stays on your device. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share data for advertising.

This policy explains what the app Fun Learn – ಕನ್ನಡ (on Android and iPhone/iPad) collects, why, and how you can delete it. The app is made for children to use with a parent, so we follow the Google Play Families policy and the rules for children's apps on the App Store.

1. Who the app is for

The app is made for young children learning Kannada. An adult (parent or guardian) signs in and sets up the app. Parent-only areas – the Parent corner, plans, payments, links that leave the app and account deletion – sit behind a parent check (a sum only a grown-up can answer).

3. Payments

Premium plans are bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store. They handle the payment; we never see or store card or bank details. Their own privacy policies apply to the payment.

4. What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no ad or tracking SDKs.
  • No analytics or behaviour tracking of children.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data with anyone for marketing.
  • No chat, social features or ways for children to share information publicly.

5. Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Storage) to sign in, keep the subscription record and deliver the videos. See Firebase privacy.
  • GitHub to deliver some videos, pictures and voice clips.
  • Google Play Billing and Apple App Store for purchases.

When the app downloads a video or picture, these services receive the technical details any web request carries (such as the device's IP address). We do not use this to identify or track anyone.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your account and subscription record while your account exists. When you delete your account, they are deleted (see below). Progress on the device is removed when you delete the account or uninstall the app.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Parent corner → Delete account. This deletes your sign-in account, your subscription record and all progress on that device straight away.
  • By email: see Delete your account.

Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play or the App Store so you are not charged again.

8. Your rights

A parent can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about their family, or ask questions about this policy, by writing to us. We reply within 30 days.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules allow each family to read only its own record.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. For important changes we will also tell you in the app.
