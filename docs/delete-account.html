Send an email to ramesh.katru01@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Fun Learn account", from the email address you signed in with. We delete the account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (name, email address, user ID)
  • Your subscription record (plan, status, renewal date, order IDs, payment dates and amounts)
  • All learning progress and settings on the device (in-app deletion)

What is kept

We keep nothing after deletion. Google Play or the App Store may keep their own purchase records as the law requires.

Note: deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play or the App Store first so you are not charged again.
