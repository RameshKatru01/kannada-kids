Account & plans

How do I get to the Parent corner?

Open the Dashboard tab and tap Parents at the top. Answer the grown-up sum to open it.

What is free, and what does Gold (Premium) add?

Free: the first letter of each section, the Letter Box, the Vowels videos, level 1 of the monster games, one run a day of Musical Balloon Pop and Letter Dash, and the first worksheets. Gold opens every letter, video, game level and worksheet. Plans: ₹99 a month, ₹499 for 6 months or ₹899 a year.

I paid, but Gold isn't showing (or I got a new phone)
  1. Sign in to the app with the same account you used before.
  2. Make sure the phone's Play Store (or App Store) uses the account that paid.
  3. Go to Parent corner → See plans → Restore purchases.

Still not working? Email us with the date of the payment and its order number (it starts with GPA.) from the receipt email.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store: Google Play → Subscriptions or, on iPhone/iPad, App Store → Subscriptions. Gold stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app or the account does not cancel it.

How do I delete my account and data?

Parent corner → Delete account, or see Delete your account.

Learning

Will other languages be added?

Yes. Hindi and more Indian languages are on the way. They will appear in Parent corner → Learning language when ready, and each language keeps its own progress.

Some sections are locked. How do they open?

Sections open step by step as your child learns. To start from a later section, or open everything, use Parent corner → Learning path.

Can I change the child's name shown on the home screen?

Yes: tap the round letter at the top left of Home to open Profile, then type it in Learner Name.

Tracing says "Not yet". What does it check?

After tapping Done, at least 80% of the grey letter needs to be coloured. The parts still missing show in orange; colour them in and tap Done again.

Videos & worksheets

Videos don't play or keep loading
  • Check the phone is online. Each video is saved on the phone after it plays once, so later it works offline.
  • Close and reopen the app, and make sure it's up to date.
  • Still stuck? Email us the name of the folder and video.
How do I print worksheets?

Open a letter in Letter Box (or a section's letter list) and tap Print. The first time needs internet; after that the sheets are kept on the phone.

Privacy

Are there ads? What do you collect?

No ads and no tracking. We keep only the parent's sign-in details and the subscription record; your child's progress stays on the phone. See the privacy policy.
